## 1. Vocabulary

- **Game:** a title from the public game catalog, with its cover, description, platforms, critic score and typical play times. [code]
- **Library:** the games a person has added. Each game in it has exactly one status. [code]
- **Status:** one of three.
  - **Want:** not started.
  - **Playing:** in progress.
  - **Done:** stopped. A Done game can carry a private reason: **Finished** (reached the end) or **Let go** (stopped on purpose). [code]
- **Up Next:** an ordered queue of up to 5 games the person intends to play next. It can hold Want games, and Done games meant for a replay. [code]
- **Session:** one sitting of play that the person logs, with a length in hours and an optional note. **Time played** is the sum of a game's session lengths. [code]
- **Note:** a free-text journal entry about a game. A session can carry one. [code]
- **Log:** the sheet for recording a session or a note. It opens from the bottom bar, the sidebar, Home rows and the game page. Its button text is "Log time". [code]
- **Typical play time:** how long a game usually takes, from the game catalog. The app shows two figures, labelled "Main story" and "100%". [code]
- **Evening:** a new unit for game length. One evening is about 1.75 hours of play. The product definition already promises length shown as "about N evenings", but nothing shows it yet. [user] [code]
- **Critic score:** the catalog's 0–100 average of critic reviews. [code]
- **Cover tint:** a soft background color taken from a game's cover art. It is new; nothing in the app derives color from covers today. [user] [code]
- **Accent:** the brand color, plum. It is reserved for the main button, the selected item, the Playing status, focus outlines and links. [code]
- **Home:** the first screen after signing in. [code]
- **Game page:** the page for one game. Anyone can open it. Its personal parts appear only when the visitor is signed in and has the game in their library. [code]
- **Library page:** the list of every game in the person's library, as one card per game. The list can be filtered by status or Up Next. [code]

## 2. What it does today

**Home** [code]
- Top to bottom:
  1. The title "Home" and an "Add game" button.
  2. The Up Next shelf.
  3. A counts line: "28 games" with a pill each for Want, Playing and Done.
  4. The Playing list. Each row has a cover, title, platform and a "Log" button.
- The Up Next shelf:
  - The first cover is larger and labelled "Play next".
  - Every cover carries a status badge, so most say "Want".
  - Under each cover sit "Up Next #n" (or "Replay") and the platform.
- Playing games are ordered by when the library entry last changed. Logging a session does not move a game up.
- Nothing on Home shows how long any game is.

**Game page** [code]
- Header: a blurred screenshot as backdrop, then the cover, a small uppercase line (year · publisher · genre) and the title.
- Below the header:
  - A row of jump tabs (About, Themes, Notes, Screenshots, Related).
  - A large screenshot grid.
  - A grid of cards in uneven sizes: Rating with History, Typical play time (hours, in a typewriter-style font), About, Themes/Genres/Platforms, Notes, Related games.
- On a computer, a side column holds:
  - The status control with "Mark done", a "⋯" menu and "Log time".
  - A large critic score ring labelled "Critic consensus".
  - A "Time played / Sessions" card.
- On a phone, the status control and critic ring sit in the header, and a bar at the bottom holds the status control and "Log time".

**Library page** [code]
- Each card shows:
  - The cover, with a status badge on it and a "⋯" menu in its corner.
  - The title.
  - "Up Next #n" or "Not owned" when they apply.
  - The platform, and the rating when there is one.
  - A status control (the status badge again, with a down arrow) next to a next-step button ("Start playing", "Mark done" or "Play again").
- On a phone, cards are rows with a small cover. On a computer, they are a grid of covers.
- Nothing on a card shows how long the game is.

**Marking a game Done** [code]
- A dialog opens titled "{Game} is done". It offers Finished or Let go (neither pre-selected), a rating, "What stayed with you?", and Skip / Save.

## 3. What it depends on

- **The game catalog**, for covers, critic score, platforms, screenshots, related games and typical play times. Play times are fetched each time a game page opens; they are not kept with the game. About half of games have no play time at all. [code] [web]
- **The person's logged sessions and notes**, for time played, session count and the date of the last session. [code]
- **Light, Dark or System theme.** Every new color must work in both. [code]
- **Earlier decisions this work must respect or deliberately change:** the vocabulary and Home rules of the "mindful core" spec (026), and the accent, cover, status-chip and phone rules of the design-reset spec (027). [code]

## 4. What it changes

- **Home:**
  - Opens with one question about tonight's game instead of a title.
  - The Up Next shelf becomes simpler and shows the first game's length in evenings.
  - The Playing list becomes "Also playing".
  - Counts become a quiet line.
  - Only the person sees this. [user]
- **Game page:**
  - New header with the cover tint, title, three key facts and the actions.
  - A "Your play" card comes first.
  - About absorbs the play-time and tag cards.
  - Screenshots and related games become sideways strips.
  - Jump tabs, the side column, the critic ring and the time-played card are removed.
  - Anyone who opens the page sees the new layout; the personal parts stay private. [user]
- **Library page:** cards show the game's length in evenings and get the same cover treatment as Home and the game page. [user] What "the same cover treatment" means for a card is open question 10.
- **Game length** reads "About N evenings" wherever it is shown. Time played stays in hours. [user]
- **Status badges** no longer appear on Up Next covers. [user]
- **No stored data changes for the person.** "Not tonight" remembers nothing. [user]

## 5. Quirks

1. **"Main story" may not be the main story.**
   - **Normally:** "Main story" means time to the credits.
   - **Here:** the app reads the catalog figure that, by the catalog's usual meaning, includes some side content. This could not be confirmed from the catalog's documentation.
   - Why it matters: evenings are computed from one figure, so the wrong one inflates every length. [code] [web]
2. **Length is hidden from non-owners.**
   - **Normally:** a game's length helps decide whether to add it.
   - **Here:** play times appear only when the visitor has the game in their library.
   - Why it matters: "About N evenings" in the new header is most useful before adding. [code]
3. **Logging doesn't reorder Playing.**
   - **Normally:** "continue" lists put the most recently played game first.
   - **Here:** the order follows the last edit to the library entry. Logging a session doesn't count.
   - Why it matters: the question must ask about the right game first. [code]
4. **Two Add buttons.**
   - **Normally:** one Add button for a game you don't have.
   - **Here:** a signed-in visitor sees two, an invite card and the header control.
   - Why it matters: the new header should hold the only one. [code]
5. **Empty time card.**
   - **Normally:** "Time played 0 h" appears only for your own games.
   - **Here:** it appears for any signed-in visitor, even when the game isn't in their library.
   - Why it matters: it is being removed, and its replacement must not repeat this. [code]
6. **Back always goes to Library.**
   - **Normally:** Back returns to where you came from.
   - **Here:** the phone Back button and breadcrumbs always go to Library, and the phone "⋯" button does nothing.
   - Why it matters: the new header keeps these controls. [code]
7. **"Log a session" is a banned phrase.**
   - **Normally:** a design's button wording is adopted.
   - **Here:** the mindful-core spec fixed "Log time" as the only wording and lists "Log a session" as never to be used, while the reference design uses it.
   - Why it matters: one of the two must give way. [code] [user]
8. **Covers may not color the interface.**
   - **Normally:** a redesign can add colors.
   - **Here:** the design-reset spec says every color comes from the fixed palette (covers are excluded only as images), and the accent appears in five places only.
   - Why it matters: the cover tint is a deliberate exception and must say so. [code]
9. **The shelf is promised the top spot.**
   - **Normally:** a new first element simply goes on top.
   - **Here:** two earlier specs require the Up Next shelf to be the first, largest element and visible without scrolling on a 390 × 844 phone.
   - Why it matters: the question takes that place. [code]
10. **Choosing Done opens a dialog.**
    - **Normally:** choosing Finished or Let go is the last step.
    - **Here:** marking Done always opens a dialog that asks for Finished/Let go, a rating and a reflection.
    - Why it matters: "Done with it?" → Finished / Let go in the header must decide what happens to that dialog. [code]
11. **A library card shows its status twice.**
    - **Normally:** one status per card.
    - **Here:** each card has a status badge on the cover and a status control with the same badge below the title.
    - Why it matters: the new cover treatment removes badges from covers, so one of the two goes. [code]

## 6. Open questions

1. **Which play time becomes evenings?** Assumption: the figure shown today as "Main story", relabelled to match whatever it really measures once confirmed.
2. **Button wording:** "Log a session" (reference design) or "Log time" (mindful-core vocabulary)? Assumption: keep "Log time" everywhere, including the Home question, so one word means one thing.
3. **Length for non-owners:** show "About N evenings" in the header to everyone? Assumption: yes. The facts row shows to every visitor, because length matters most before adding.
4. **"Done with it?" and the Done dialog:** does picking Finished or Let go in the header skip the dialog? Assumption: it marks the game Done with that reason and still opens the dialog for an optional rating and reflection, with the reason already chosen.
5. **Order of the question:** "last session" means the date of the most recent logged session. A Playing game with no sessions yet counts by the date it became Playing. Assumption: as stated.
6. **Home with no Playing and no Up Next games:** what does the question panel show? Assumption: no question. The existing empty states stay ("Nothing playing right now" with "Pick from Up Next" / "Add a game").
7. **Covers that give no clear color** (greyscale or mostly white): assumption: the surface uses the normal neutral card color.
8. **Games with no play time:** assumption: the Length fact is left out of the facts row, and the row shows the other facts.
9. **Desktop Home:** the reference design shows the question on phone only. Assumption: desktop Home gets the same question panel at the top, and keeps its page title "Home" because the sidebar has room.
10. **Cover treatment on library cards:** what changes? Assumption:
    - The status badge leaves the cover. The status control below the title is the only place status appears.
    - Cards are not tinted, so a screen keeps one tinted surface at most and the library stays calm.
11. **Length on library cards:** where and when? Assumption:
    - Every card shows "About N evenings" under the title.
    - Cards for games with no known play time show nothing there.
    - Playing and Done games show it too, because length never changes as you play.
